What does LeadsMap help collect?

LeadsMap helps organize visible Google Maps business result details such as business names, categories, addresses, phone numbers, websites, ratings, and map links.

What should I check before outreach?

Review the exported spreadsheet, remove duplicates, segment records by niche and city, and avoid publishing private or sensitive data in public support channels.

Where can I ask support questions?

Use the GitHub support repository at github.com/webAutomationLover/leadsmap or the public support discussion at LeadsMap support links and workflow discussion.

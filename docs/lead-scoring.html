Scoring Google Maps Leads After Export

A raw Google Maps export is easier to use when each business record receives a simple priority score before outreach.

This public note is for teams using LeadsMap to collect visible local business details and then rank records for follow-up.

Deduping Google Maps leads

Simple score fields

  • Category match: whether the business matches the campaign niche.
  • Contact readiness: whether the record includes a phone number and website.
  • Local fit: whether the address is inside the target city or service area.
  • Review signal: whether ratings and review counts suggest an active listing.
  • Manual confidence: whether a teammate can quickly verify the Maps URL.

Scoring workflow

  1. Export one focused Google Maps search with LeadsMap.
  2. Review duplicate domains, missing websites, and incomplete phone fields.
  3. Add a small priority score before assigning records to outreach.
  4. Keep the original Google Maps URL so the source can be checked later.

A lightweight scoring pass helps teams prioritize local business records without overcomplicating the lead list workflow.

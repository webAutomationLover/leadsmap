Google Maps Lead Data Cleanup Notes for LeadsMap

This public note focuses on the cleanup steps that happen after exporting Google Maps business results with LeadsMap.

Official LeadsMap website

LeadsMap install guide

Google Maps lead export checklist

Columns to review

  • Business name: remove duplicates and obvious non-business results.
  • Website: group records with the same domain before importing into a CRM.
  • Category and address: keep the niche and location filters consistent.
  • Map link: preserve it when a teammate needs to verify the listing later.

Cleanup reminders

Segment records by city, niche, and website status before outreach. Keep private outreach notes in your own workspace rather than public documentation pages.

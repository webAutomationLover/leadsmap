Google Maps lead list workflow

Last reviewed: July 4, 2026.

Before export

  • Use a specific service keyword and city or neighborhood in the Google Maps search.
  • Confirm the visible results match the niche you want to research.
  • Keep each export focused so categories and locations stay easy to filter later.

After export

  • Remove duplicate business names, repeated websites, and irrelevant categories.
  • Check missing websites or phone numbers before importing the list into another tool.
  • Keep private notes and outreach history out of public support pages.

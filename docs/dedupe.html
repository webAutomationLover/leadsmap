Deduping Google Maps Leads Before CRM Import

Local lead research works better when duplicate businesses are caught before a spreadsheet is assigned to sales, marketing, or operations teammates.

These public notes describe a simple dedupe pass for Google Maps lead exports collected with LeadsMap.

Google Maps lead data cleanup notes

Common duplicate signals

  • The same website domain appears across several Google Maps listings.
  • A business has multiple branch locations that should be grouped under one account.
  • Phone numbers or addresses repeat with slightly different business names.
  • Directory pages, social profiles, or placeholder websites appear instead of a business-owned domain.

Review steps

  1. Sort exported records by website domain, then by phone number.
  2. Keep the original Google Maps URL so each record can be checked later.
  3. Tag branch locations separately from true duplicate listings.
  4. Move uncertain records into a review tab instead of deleting them immediately.

A short dedupe pass keeps outreach lists cleaner without turning a lightweight Google Maps research workflow into a heavy data-cleaning project.
